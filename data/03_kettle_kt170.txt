# Manual: BaanSuk Electric Kettle KT-170

Specifications: KT-170 has a capacity of 1.7 liters and a power rating of 2,200 W. It has a stainless steel body, a 360-degree cordless base, auto shut-off when water boils, and boil-dry protection. Boiling 1 liter of water takes about 4 minutes.

How to use: Fill water between the MIN (0.5 L) and MAX (1.7 L) marks. Never overfill, as boiling water may spill from the spout. Place the kettle on its base, close the lid, and press the switch down. The blue light turns on. The kettle switches off automatically when the water boils.

Keep warm function: Press the KEEP WARM button after boiling to hold the water at 90 degrees Celsius for up to 30 minutes.

Descaling: If you see white scale inside, fill the kettle halfway with water and add 2 tablespoons of white vinegar or 1 teaspoon of citric acid. Boil, then leave for 15 minutes, pour out, and rinse with clean water twice. Descale every 4 weeks if you use tap water. Do not use chemical drain cleaners.

Troubleshooting: If the kettle does not turn on, check that it is seated correctly on the base and that the water level is above MIN. If it switches off early, there may be heavy scale; descale it. If it keeps clicking off, let it cool for 5 minutes because the boil-dry protection may have been triggered.

Safety: Do not immerse the kettle or base in water. Do not open the lid while boiling. Keep out of reach of children. If the power cord is damaged, stop using it and contact a BaanSuk service center.
